What We Can Fix

✓ Fixable at the Help Desk

  • Physical damage — repairs coordinated, loaner provided
  • Charging problems
  • Keyboard language settings
  • Timezone configuration

✕ Cannot Be Fixed Here

  • Login problems (Google, Schoology, HAC)
  • Classroom application issues
  • GoGuardian filters and blocks

For these, submit a tech ticket (see below).

Resource available: Chromebook Basic Troubleshooting Guide — ask staff at the Help Desk for the Google Drive link.

Help Desk FAQ

Can I get a loaner device for testing?
Approximately 60% of students use personal devices, which makes it challenging to guarantee loaner availability specifically for testing situations. Ask ahead of time if possible.
My issue isn't something the Help Desk can fix — what do I do?
Submit a tech ticket at masonohioschools.freshservice.com, or email IT Support at HSITSupport@freshservice.masonohioschools.com. Students and families can also use support@masonohioschools.com.
What happens if my Chromebook is broken?
It receives an assessment at the Help Desk, then transfers to off-site repairs with a loaner provided in the meantime. Repair costs may appear on your PaySchools Central student fees.
I forgot my Chromebook at home — can I get one for the day?
Single-bell loaner availability only; devices must be returned between class periods.

Need Help Now?

Stop by the Help Desk in the Dream Center during open hours, or submit a ticket for login/classroom app issues.
