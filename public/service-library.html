Print Books

Browse our physical book collection through the main library catalog. You'll find fiction, nonfiction, and resources to support both learning and leisure reading.

Access the catalog at masonohio.follettdestiny.com

Follett Destiny FAQ

Do I need to log in to browse?
No, browsing access doesn't need a login.
How many items can I check out?
Up to 5 items per student.
How long is the loan period?
14 days.
Can I renew a book?
Yes, if there are no holds on it.
Can I place a hold on a book?
Yes, request a hold at the help desk.
Are there late fees?
No late fees are charged.

Digital Books

Our digital catalog gives you access to eBooks and audiobooks that you can read or listen to anytime, anywhere via the Sora app or website.

Access Sora at soraapp.com/library/masonscdoh

Sora FAQ

How do I log in?
Use your school Google account.
What platforms does Sora work on?
The Sora app (iOS/Android) or any web browser.
How long is the loan period?
2-3 weeks, and books return automatically — no late fees possible.
Can I read offline?
Yes, titles are downloadable for offline access.
Can I transfer a book to my Kindle?
Yes, via the in-app directions for Kindle transfer.
