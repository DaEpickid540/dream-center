Express 3D & Poster Printing

Transform your designs into reality with professional printing services

What We Offer

3D Printing

Bring your CAD designs to life with professional 3D printing. Perfect for prototypes, art projects, and engineering models.

Capabilities:
  • Multiple material options
  • Various sizes and complexities
  • Post-processing available
  • Design consultation

Poster Printing

High-quality color printing for presentations, announcements, and displays. Professional results every time.

Available Options:
  • Multiple sizes (8x10 to 24x36)
  • Matte or glossy finishes
  • Rush orders available
  • Design help available

How to Order

Step 1: Prepare Your Files

Bring your design files in digital format (STL, PDF, JPG, PNG, etc.). Our staff can help optimize files if needed.

Step 2: Discuss Options

Talk with our team about materials, sizes, finishes, and timelines. We'll help you choose the best options.

Step 3: Approve & Pay

Review estimates and provide payment (if applicable). Most services are free for school projects.

Step 4: Pickup

Your finished prints will be ready! Pickup during open hours at the Dream Center.

Pricing & Timing

Most services are included in your school experience. Check with staff for any material costs.

Standard Timeline:

Posters: 1-2 school days

3D Prints: 2-5 school days (depending on complexity)

Express Service: Available for rush projects
