Poster Basics

Our large-format printer produces high-quality, oversized posters from digital files you submit.

File Requirements

  • Accepted formats: PNG, JPG, PDF
  • General size guideline: 18 × 24" or smaller
  • Files must be the exact size needed — we cannot resize images for you
  • Limit yourself to one print per project

Design Tools

Beginner: Canva Adobe Express Advanced: Photoshop Illustrator InDesign

3D Printing Basics

Our printers create physical objects by building them layer by layer from melted filament.

File Requirements

  • Accepted formats: STL and 3MF
  • Files must be fully prepared and ready to print — we cannot make design changes for you
  • Materials available: PLA and PET-G
  • Color requests generally aren't possible — colors rotate based on current filament stock

Frequently Asked Questions

Poster Printer FAQ

What design software can I use?
Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, Adobe Express, and Canva all work well for poster design.
How many posters can I print?
We ask that students limit themselves to one print per project.
What size should my poster be?
18 x 24" or smaller is our general guideline for poster sizes.

3D Printer FAQ

What printers does the Dream Center have?
4 Bambu Carbon X-1 printers and 2 Bambu A-1 printers, both from Bambu Labs.
Can I choose the filament color?
Because of the volume of prints we deal with, we do not generally take color requests. Colors rotate based on current stock.
Which file format should I submit?
STL files are standard. Advanced users may submit .3mf or .gcode files.
What's the biggest object you can print?
Our printers can handle 256mm in any direction. Large projects that use a lot of filament may not be possible.
How do I export from Tinkercad?
Select your object(s) and export as .STL format.
Please be sure to read the printing rules on the submission form for more information before you submit.
