Why Peer Tutoring Works

Relatable Explanations

Your peer tutors remember struggling with the same concepts. They explain things in ways that make sense to students.

Comfortable Learning

No judgment, no pressure. A supportive peer tutoring session creates the perfect learning environment.

Personal Connection

Build relationships with other students who care about learning and success.

Flexible Scheduling

Work around your schedule. Sessions can happen during study hall, lunch, or after school.

How to Get Tutoring

Step 1: Visit

Stop by the Dream Center and tell our staff what subject you need help with.

Step 2: Match

We'll match you with a knowledgeable peer tutor who can help.

Step 3: Schedule

Pick a time that works for both you and your tutor.

Step 4: Learn

Get the help you need and build confidence in your subject!
