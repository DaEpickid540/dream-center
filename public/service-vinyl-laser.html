Because there are many variables to producing good vinyl and laser cut objects, we ask that you schedule a consultation with one of our student interns rather than walking up to the machines directly.

Our Equipment

Cricut Venture Vinyl Cutter

Precision cutting of materials like vinyl, cardstock, and iron-on transfers. Enables custom stickers, decals, and T-shirt designs.

  • Works with Cricut Design Space software
  • Compatible with vinyl, paper, and heat transfer film

xTool P2S CO2 Laser Cutter

Cuts, engraves, and etches a wide range of materials with precision.

  • Compatible with wood, acrylic, leather, and glass
  • Processes vector and image-based designs
  • Enclosed cutting area for safety

Key Requirements

Do I need the correct file type before I arrive?
Yes — having your file prepared correctly (see our tutorials above) makes your consultation much faster.
Does the Dream Center provide materials?
No, visitors must bring their own materials for both vinyl and laser projects.
Who do I contact directly about a laser or vinyl project?
Email robertsa@masonohioschools.com to schedule or ask questions.
