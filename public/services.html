Express 3D & Poster Printing

Quick-turnaround digital fabrication and printing services. Perfect for projects, presentations, and creative displays.

What We Offer:

  • 3D Printing: Create three-dimensional objects from digital CAD files
  • Poster Printing: High-quality color printing for presentations and displays
  • Custom Projects: Work with staff to realize your ideas in physical form

How to Request:

Submit your design files to a Dream Center staff member. We can help with file preparation, material selection, and printing timelines. Express service available!

Perfect For:

Science projects • Art portfolios • Club materials • Personal creations • Engineering prototypes

✂️

Vinyl & Laser Cutting Services

Professional vinyl cutting and laser engraving for custom projects, personalized items, and precision work.

What We Offer:

  • Vinyl Cutting: Create custom decals, stickers, and cut designs for clothing and more
  • Laser Engraving: Personalize items with precision engraving on wood, acrylic, leather, and other materials
  • Design Support: Help with file conversion and optimization for cutting

How to Request:

Bring your design or we can help you create one. Submit digital files (SVG, PDF, or AI formats work best) to schedule your cutting session.

Perfect For:

Custom decals • Personalized gifts • T-shirt designs • Acrylic crafts • Engraved awards & plaques

Dream Center Library

Our curated collection of resources, materials, and equipment to support your creative projects.

What's Available:

  • Maker material supplies (fabric, wood, acrylic, leather)
  • Reference books and guides for makers and creators
  • Shared equipment and tools
  • Design software and digital resources
  • Project inspiration and idea collections

How to Access:

Browse our collection during open hours. Ask staff members for recommendations based on your project needs.

Check Out Materials:

Many items can be checked out for use in your projects. Check with staff about checkout policies.

💻

Chromebook Help & Support

Technical support and troubleshooting for your school Chromebook. Get help when you need it most.

Common Issues We Help With:

  • Login and account problems
  • App installation and troubleshooting
  • Connectivity issues (WiFi, Google Classroom, etc.)
  • File management and cloud sync
  • Browser extensions and tools
  • General device settings and optimization

How to Get Help:

Stop by the Dream Center during open hours. Our tech-savvy staff can usually resolve issues quickly, or we'll help connect you with IT support if needed.

Tips:

Before visiting, try restarting your Chromebook or logging out and logging back in. Sometimes these simple steps resolve issues!

Cosmic Cafe

Fuel your creativity with food and refreshments. Grab a bite while you work on your projects.

What's Available:

  • Coffee and hot beverages
  • Snacks and light refreshments
  • Meals during school hours
  • Special treats and seasonal offerings

Hours:

Available every school bell during regular school hours. Perfect timing to grab something before or after your Dream Center visit!

Why Visit:

The Cosmic Cafe is the perfect spot to relax, refuel, and connect with other makers and creators. Many of our best collaborations happen over coffee!

👥

Peer Tutoring

Get academic support from fellow students who understand what you're going through. Expert help with any subject or skill.

Subjects Covered:

  • Math (all levels)
  • Science (Biology, Chemistry, Physics, etc.)
  • English & Writing
  • History & Social Studies
  • Foreign Languages
  • Test Prep (ACT, SAT, AP exams)
  • Study Skills & Organization

How to Request Tutoring:

Visit the Dream Center and speak with staff about your tutoring needs. We'll match you with a peer tutor who can help with your specific subject or challenge.

Why Peer Tutoring:

Peer tutors speak your language. They've been where you are and can explain concepts in ways that make sense. Plus, it's a great way to connect with classmates and build community!
