All Services

🖨️

Express 3D & Poster Printing

Quick-turnaround printing for 3D models and high-quality posters. Perfect for presentations, projects, and creative displays.

What We Offer:
  • 3D Printing: Transform CAD designs into tangible objects
  • Poster Printing: High-resolution color printing in various sizes
  • Express Service: Quick turnaround for urgent projects
How to Request:

Bring your design files to the Dream Center. Our staff will help with file prep, material selection, and timeline planning.

✂️

Vinyl & Laser Cutting

Professional vinyl cutting and laser engraving for custom decals, stickers, and personalized items with precision.

Capabilities:
  • Vinyl Cutting: Custom decals, stickers, t-shirt designs, personalized items
  • Laser Engraving: Wood, acrylic, leather, metal engraving with precision
  • Design Support: Help optimizing files for cutting
Perfect For:

Custom decals • Personalized gifts • Apparel design • Awards & plaques • Art projects

📚

Resource Library

Our curated collection of materials, equipment, and resources to support your creative projects.

Available Resources:
  • Maker materials (fabric, wood, acrylic, leather)
  • Reference guides and maker handbooks
  • Shared tools and equipment
  • Design software and digital resources
  • Project inspiration galleries
How to Access:

Visit the Dream Center during open hours. Browse our collection and ask staff for personalized recommendations based on your project.

💻

Chromebook Support

Technical support and troubleshooting for your school Chromebook. Get help fast from our tech-savvy team.

We Help With:
  • Login and account issues
  • App installation and troubleshooting
  • WiFi and connectivity problems
  • Google Classroom and productivity apps
  • Device settings and optimization
Quick Tip:

Try restarting your Chromebook or logging out/back in first—these simple fixes often solve problems!

Peer Tutoring

Expert academic support from fellow students. Get help with any subject in a comfortable, collaborative setting.

Subjects Available:
  • Math • Science • English • History
  • Foreign Languages • Test Prep
  • Study Skills • Organization
Why Peer Tutoring?

Peer tutors understand student perspectives and explain concepts in relatable ways. It's a great way to build academic confidence and community!
