Top 25 Ideas for 3D Printing

Tips & Tricks: inspiration organized by category

Getting Started with 3D Printing Submit a Model for Printing

School & Learning

  1. Custom rulers, protractors, or math manipulatives
  2. Science models (molecules, DNA helix, volcano cross-sections)
  3. Geography tools (3D maps, topographic models, globes)
  4. History artifacts (replicas of ancient coins, pottery, or tools)
  5. Personalized bookmarks for reading projects

Everyday Useful Items

  1. Keychains or name tags
  2. Phone stands or cord organizers
  3. Pencil holders or desk organizers
  4. Earbud cases or headphone stands
  5. Cable clips or monitor hooks

Creative Fun

  1. Mini toys or figurines
  2. Puzzle cubes or brain teasers
  3. Board game pieces or dice
  4. Costume props (masks, armor parts, cosplay add-ons)
  5. Spinning tops or fidget toys

Personalized Gifts

  1. Custom jewelry (pendants, rings, bracelets)
  2. Candle holders or decorative vases
  3. Cake toppers for birthdays or events
  4. Awards, medals, or trophies
  5. Picture frames with unique designs

STEM & Maker Projects

  1. Gears, wheels, or mechanical parts for experiments
  2. Mini cars, boats, or rockets (with simple moving parts)
  3. Tool holders or custom jig helpers
  4. Architectural models or building prototypes
  5. Prosthetic parts, assistive devices, or accessibility tools (in collaboration with real-world initiatives)

Found Your Idea?

Design or find your model, then submit it as STL or 3MF.
