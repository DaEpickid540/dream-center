Understand the Process

You'll create (or find) a 3D model file, then submit it to the Dream Center's Express Printing Service — staff handle the actual printing rather than students operating the machines directly.

Find or Create a Model

Browse Free Models

Thingiverse, Printables, or Tinkercad Gallery for existing designs.

Beginner Design

Tinkercad for beginner-friendly, web-based design.

Advanced Design

SketchUp for architectural work, or Fusion 360/Blender for advanced projects.

Design Tips

  • Keep it simple and avoid fragile elements
  • Incorporate flat bases for stability
  • Ensure walls measure at least 2mm thick
  • Limit overhangs to 45 degrees unless you're planning support structures

Prepare Your File

  1. Export your model as STL or 3MF format
  2. Verify scaling before submission
  3. Complete the Dream Center's 3D Printing Request Form
