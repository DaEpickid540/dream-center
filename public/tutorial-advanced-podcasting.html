Next Level Podcasting

Tips & Tricks: mic technique, room treatment, and publishing

Mic Distance
6–8 inches from your mouth
Loop Browser

Export
MP3 to cloud storage

Anchor or major directories

1. Mic Setup

Keep the mic 6–8 inches from your mouth, and speak slightly past the mic (not directly into it) to reduce popping sounds.

2. Control the Space

Record in a quiet room — turn off fans, notifications, and background noise. Soft furnishings like carpets or blankets help absorb echo and improve audio quality.

3. Be a Clear Communicator

Smile while speaking — it makes your voice sound brighter. Use natural pauses instead of "um" or "uh."

4. Smart Editing

Remove extended quiet moments and verbal stumbles, and balance voices so no speaker sounds louder than the rest.

5. Add Music in GarageBand

For an intro or outro, open the Loop Browser (O key) and drag music into a new track. Position it strategically and lower the track volume so music doesn't overpower voices.

6. Publishing Your Podcast

  • Export as MP3 to cloud storage
  • Upload to platforms like Anchor
  • Submit to major podcast directories
  • Or simply share audio files with classmates for school assignments
