How to Create and Size a Button Graphic in Canva

Getting Started: Button Making

Tool
Canva.com
Print Setting
100% scale — do not resize to fit page
1" Button
Design at 1.25 × 1.25 in
2.25" Button
Design at 2.5 × 2.5 in

Step-by-Step

Step 1: Open Canva and Set Up Your Design

  1. Go to Canva.com and log in with Google
  2. Select Create a design → Custom size
  3. Enter dimensions with bleed area:
    • 1-inch button: 1.25 × 1.25 inches
    • 2.25-inch button: 2.5 × 2.5 inches
    • 3-inch button: 3.25 × 3.25 inches

Step 2: Design Your Button

  1. Add shapes and icons via the Elements toolbar
  2. Click Text to add wording and adjust font, color, and size
  3. Use Uploads for personal images or logos
  4. Keep key elements within the safe zone (approximately 2.25 inches centered)

Step 3: Design Tips

Add a guide circle showing the final visible area after crimping, and avoid placing text or graphics near the edges — the crimping process will cut them off.

Step 4: Download

  1. Click Share → Download and choose PNG or PDF Print
  2. Disable the transparent background unless you specifically need it

Step 5: Print and Cut

  1. Print at 100% scale — do not resize to fit the page
  2. Use a circle cutter or scissors to trim your design

Step 6: Assemble

  1. Follow the American Button Machines instructions
  2. Place your design between the mylar and metal parts
  3. Press to crimp
