Overview

Canva is a great tool to help you create graphics that will eventually become vinyl cuts on the Cricut machine.

Part 1 — Design Your Graphic in Canva

  1. Launch Canva and select a custom 12×12 inch design size
  2. Activate rulers and guides through the View menu for proper alignment
  3. Construct designs using basic shapes (rectangles, circles, hearts, stars) while maintaining solid fills
  4. Combine shapes using Unite or Subtract functions to reduce complexity
  5. Prioritize filled shapes over thin outlines for optimal cutting results
  6. Group related elements together and assign descriptive names

Part 2 — Export as SVG

  1. Navigate to Share → Download and select SVG as the file format
  2. Verify only necessary page content is included; deselect crop marks and bleed
Note: SVG download works only in Canva Pro.

Ready to Cut?

The Dream Center encourages scheduling time with staff for your first Cricut Venture cut.

Troubleshooting

My import came in fragmented
Return to Canva and unite shapes before exporting again.
Letters with holes (like O or A) came out hollow
May need Transfer Tape, or a structural redesign that bridges the gap.
Thin elements aren't cutting well
Thicken the lines/shapes, or adjust cut speed with staff during your session.
My result looks pixelated
This usually means you downloaded a PNG instead of an SVG export — re-export as SVG.

Pro Tips for Classroom Success

  • Keep designs simple
  • Stick to a single color per cut
  • Run a small test cut before committing to your full material
  • Create a reusable template for repeat projects
  • Document your finished projects for next time
