Green Screening with iMovie

Tips & Tricks: replace your background, keep your subject

Room
Dream Center Green Screen Room
Equipment
Tripods with phone cages, lighting rigs, lapel mics
Editing Software
iMovie (on the iMacs)
File Transfer
AirDrop (iPhone) or Google Drive

Overview

Record video in our Green Screen Room using your personal phone or one of our cameras, then replace the green background with your own photos, Google Slides, or video clips!

First Steps

Start by recording some video! The room contains tripods with phone cages, lighting rigs, and lapel microphones.

Helpful Resources

Beginners Guide to Studio Lights (YouTube) Beginners Guide to Bluetooth Lapel Mics (YouTube)

Transfer Your Footage

From an iPhone

Use AirDrop to send your video directly to the iMac.

From Any Device

Save your file to Google Drive and open it on the iMac.

Replace the Background in iMovie

Dream Center staff are available to walk you through replacing the green background in iMovie step by step — just ask when you're ready to edit.

Ready to Shoot?

Reserve time in the Green Screen Room during open hours.
