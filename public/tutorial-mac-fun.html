Fun Things to Do on the Mac

Tips & Tricks: five built-in apps worth exploring

Find Any App
Launchpad (rocket icon) in the Dock
Quick Search
Spotlight (magnifying glass, top right)

Most built-in Apple apps feature colorful icons — like a microphone symbol for GarageBand.

1. Photo Booth — Get Creative with Photos & Videos

  • Take amusing selfies with friends using integrated filters
  • Record brief video clips with fun effects
  • Create a time-lapse sequence of your group project or workspace

2. GarageBand — Make Music or Podcasts

  • Record a quick podcast episode with a partner
  • Experiment with loops to build your own beat
  • Try playing virtual instruments like keyboard, guitar, or drums

3. Notes — Stay Organized & Creative

  • Create a checklist for school projects or Dream Center tasks
  • Sketch diagrams with the drawing tool
  • Organize class notes, project ideas, and to-dos using folders

4. Freeform — Collaborate & Brainstorm

  • Start a whiteboard and add sticky notes, shapes, and drawings
  • Invite classmates to brainstorm together in real time
  • Use it as a vision board for project planning or creative ideas

5. iMovie — Make Your Own Movies

  • Import clips from your phone or camera and edit them together
  • Add titles, transitions, and music to tell your story
  • Create a short trailer or highlight reel for a project or event

Want to go further with iMovie? Try our Green Screening with iMovie guide.
