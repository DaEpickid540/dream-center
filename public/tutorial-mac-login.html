Logging In and Out of a Mac

Login Process

  1. Enter your school email and password (formatted as "Mason+StudentID#")
  2. Press Return or click the arrow to log in

Post-Login Interface

Once logged in, you'll see three key elements:

Menu Bar

At the top, with system controls.

The Dock

At the bottom, showing frequently used apps.

Launchpad

For accessing additional apps.

iCloud (Optional)

iCloud setup is available through Apple Menu → System Settings → Apple ID, but this step remains optional for school use.

Logout Procedure

  1. Click the Apple Menu in the top left corner
  2. Select the logout option for your account
  3. Confirm if prompted
Always save your work to Google Drive so it's backed up and available on any device. Files saved only on the Mac may be lost when you log out.
