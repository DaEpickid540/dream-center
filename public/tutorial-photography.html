1. Frame Your Shot

Use the "Rule of Thirds": imagine your photo divided into 9 equal squares (like a tic-tac-toe grid). Place your subject where the lines cross for a more balanced, interesting image. Keep backgrounds simple so your subject stands out.

Video: Rule of Thirds (YouTube)

2. Light It Right

Use two lights: one at 45° to the subject (key light), one on the opposite side (fill light). Avoid harsh overhead lights — they create unflattering shadows.

3. Camera Basics

Hold steady or use a tripod for clear shots. Focus on the subject's eyes for portraits.

4. Creativity Counts

Try different angles — high, low, or side perspectives. Use reflective surfaces (white poster board or a wall) to bounce extra light.

5. Check & Edit Your Results

Take multiple shots and review them before moving on.
