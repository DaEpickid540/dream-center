Getting Started: Podcasting in GarageBand

Record, edit, and export your first episode on Mac

Software
GarageBand (Mac)
Target Length
5–15 minutes for first episodes
Split Clip
Command + T
Export Format
MP3 or AAC

How to Create a Podcast

1. Open GarageBand

  1. Click Launchpad in the Dock and look for GarageBand (guitar icon)
  2. Or use Spotlight Search (top-right corner), type GarageBand, and hit Return
  3. Select Empty Project, then choose Microphone

2. Set Up Tracks

  1. GarageBand begins with one microphone track — rename it (e.g. "Host" or "Mic 1")
  2. Add additional tracks for guests using the "+" button

3. Check Your Mic

  1. Go to GarageBand → Preferences → Audio/MIDI
  2. Choose your microphone — the built-in Mac mic or a USB mic
  3. Test by speaking; green/yellow bars should respond

4. Record Your Podcast

  1. Press the red record button to begin
  2. Speak clearly into your mic
  3. Press spacebar to stop
  4. Record each speaker on separate tracks

5. Edit Your Audio

  1. Select and trim clips by dragging edges
  2. Use Command + T to split audio
  3. Adjust volume using the line across each track

6. Add Music or Effects (Optional)

  1. Open the Loop Browser (O key) for free sounds
  2. Drag intro/outro music into your project

7. Export Your Podcast

  1. Go to Share → Export Song to Disk
  2. Choose MP3 or AAC format
  3. Save to desktop

Podcasting Tips for Beginners

Before You Record

  • Plan ahead with outlines and key points
  • Choose a quiet location
  • Test equipment, mics, cables, levels beforehand
  • Target 5–15 minutes for your first episode

During Recording

  • Speak clearly at a steady pace
  • Take turns speaking; avoid overlapping
  • Smile while recording — it conveys energy
  • Keep water nearby

Editing & Polishing

  • Remove mistakes, silences, and filler words
  • Add intro/outro music for tone
  • Balance audio levels across voices
  • Listen through headphones for detail

Sharing Your Podcast

  • Save as MP3 or AAC
  • Create a simple cover image
  • Write a brief description
  • Share with friends, teachers, or class sites
