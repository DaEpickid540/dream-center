Canva Step-by-Step

  1. Open Canva — visit www.canva.com and log in with your school Google credentials
  2. Start a custom design — click "Create a Design," select "Custom Size," then enter your width/height in inches
  3. Design — add text, upload images or use Canva's library, apply shapes and backgrounds. Keep text large and readable from a distance.
  4. Verify size — confirm your exact poster dimensions. The Dream Center cannot resize files.
  5. Export — download as PDF (Print) format and submit via the Express Printing Service

Ready to Submit?

Remember: PNG, JPG, or PDF, sized exactly as needed, one print per project.
