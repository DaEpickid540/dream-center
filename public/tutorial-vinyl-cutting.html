Understand the Process

  1. Create (or find) an image or design
  2. Schedule a consultation with Dream Center staff for final setup
  3. During the appointment, staff will adjust settings and send your design to the Cricut Venture

Find or Create a Design

Search Cricut Design Space's library or look for free SVG files on trusted sites.

Beginner

  • Start in Canva — keep shapes bold and simple
  • Export as PNG/PDF

Advanced

  • Inkscape, Figma, or Illustrator
  • Create SVG vector files (best for vinyl cutting)

Design Tips

  • Use bold shapes and clean lines — tiny details may peel or tear
  • Select thicker fonts for text so letters cut cleanly
  • Designs should be solid black/white — no colors, gradients, or shading
  • Avoid very thin lines (<1mm) — they may not cut or weed well

Prepare Your File

  1. Save your work as SVG (preferred) or high-resolution PNG/PDF
  2. Make sure all text is converted to outlines/paths so fonts don't change
