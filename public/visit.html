How to Visit

Teachers: Book a Class Visit

Bring your entire class for a collaborative maker experience. Reserve time using our Google Calendar booking system.

How It Works:
  • View the booking presentation guide
  • Access the Dream Center Google Calendar
  • Select an available time slot
  • Specify your class size and learning objectives
  • Staff will confirm your booking

Students: Pop-In Anytime

Individual students can visit during school hours with teacher permission. Perfect for personal projects or collaboration.

Access Methods:
  • SmartPass: Request "DC Pop-In" pass with teacher approval
  • Study Hall: Access with monitor approval during designated periods
  • Before/After School: Open to students outside regular class hours

Tip: Always get teacher permission first. Review SmartPass policies for details.

Clubs & Organizations

Reserve the Dream Center for club meetings, events, and after-school activities.

To Reserve:
  • Email: jschmeichel@masonohioschools.com
  • Email: mshaedig@masonohioschools.com
  • Provide club details and preferred time slots
  • Confirm a Site Manager will be present
Important: After-school bookings don't guarantee exclusive use. The space remains open to regular traffic.

Outside Groups & Tours

Schools and organizations can request a guided tour and presentation about the Dream Center.

Request a Tour:
  1. Complete the Site Visit Request Form
  2. Specify group size and preferred dates
  3. Include details about your organization
  4. Staff will confirm and coordinate logistics

Frequently Asked Questions

What if my class is too large?

We can accommodate large groups, though very big classes may need multiple sessions. Contact us to discuss the best arrangement.

Do I need training to use equipment?

Some equipment requires brief safety training. Our staff provides orientation and hands-on guidance. First-time users get a full overview.

Can I bring my own designs?

Absolutely! We welcome personal projects. Bring digital files or printed designs, and our staff will help you bring them to life.

Is there a cost?

Most services are included in your school experience. Some specialized requests or materials may have costs—ask our staff for details.

What if I get stuck?

Staff are available during all open hours to help troubleshoot, provide guidance, and support your projects. We're here to help!

Can I work on personal projects?

Yes! The Dream Center welcomes personal projects. Pop in during open hours and our team will help you realize your ideas.
