How to Visit

There are several ways to experience the Dream Center, depending on your needs and availability:

👥 Teachers: Book a Class Visit

Bring your entire class to the Dream Center for a collaborative learning experience. Teachers can reserve the Dream Center for full-class visits using Google Calendar.

How to Book:
  • Access the Dream Center Google Calendar booking system
  • Choose an available time slot that works for your class schedule
  • Submit your class size and learning objectives
  • A staff member will confirm your booking

📄 Download the step-by-step presentation: "Booking Space in the Dream Center Using Google Calendar"

📱 Students: Pop-In Visits

Individual students can visit the Dream Center during school hours with teacher permission. It's the perfect way to work on a personal project or collaborate with friends.

How to Access:
  • Via SmartPass: Get teacher permission, then use SmartPass to request a "DC Pop-In" pass
  • During Study Hall: With monitor approval, students can access during designated study hall periods
  • Before/After School: Visit before 7:20 AM or after 6:00 PM (Mon-Thu) or 3:00 PM (Fri)

Important: Always get teacher permission before requesting a pass. Review SmartPass policies with your teacher if you have questions.

🎭 Clubs & Activities: Reserve Space

Club advisors and activity organizers can book the Dream Center for after-school meetings and events.

How to Reserve:
  • Contact Jenni Schmeichel or Melissa Shaedig via district email
  • Provide your club name, expected size, and preferred time slots
  • Confirm that a Site Manager will be present for your event

⚠️ Important Note: Booking an after-school space does not provide exclusive use. The Dream Center remains open to regular student traffic during posted hours. Plan accordingly!

🏫 External Groups: Request a Tour

Schools and organizations interested in learning about the Dream Center can request an official tour and presentation.

How to Request:
  1. Complete the Site Visit Request Form
  2. Specify the group size and preferred date/time
  3. Include details about your school or organization
  4. A staff member will contact you to confirm details

Frequently Asked Questions

What if my class is too large?

We can accommodate most group sizes, but very large classes may need to be split across multiple sessions. Contact us to discuss your needs and we'll find a solution that works for everyone.

Do I need special training to use the equipment?

Some equipment requires brief training or orientation for safety reasons. Our staff will provide guidance and supervision as needed. First-time users will receive an overview of the tools and safety protocols.

Can I bring outside materials or designs?

Yes! Students are encouraged to bring personal projects. We can work with digital files, printed designs, or help you develop ideas from scratch. Check with staff about material compatibility with specific equipment.

Is there a cost to use the Dream Center?

Most services and facility access are included in your school experience. Some specialized requests or materials may have associated costs—ask our staff for details.

What happens if I need help or have questions?

Staff members are available during all open hours to provide guidance, troubleshoot problems, and help you with your projects. Don't hesitate to ask if you need assistance!

Can I work on personal projects during open hours?

Absolutely! The Dream Center welcomes personal projects. Pop in during available times, and our team will help you bring your ideas to life using our equipment and resources.
